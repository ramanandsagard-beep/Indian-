CO2 · Assignment 3

Regional Painting Fusion Project

A conceptual artwork combining the geometric storytelling of Warli with the natural motifs and narrative textile language of Kalamkari.

Warli × Kalamkari

Warli contribution: simplified human figures, circles, triangles and community scenes.

Kalamkari contribution: flowing vines, floral borders, animal motifs and narrative composition inspired by hand-painted textile traditions.

Fusion concept: a village festival beside a stylized sacred tree, with Warli-style figures placed inside a Kalamkari-inspired botanical frame.

Warli geometric figures Kalamkari botanical motifs Fusion composition

Title: “Festival Under the Sacred Tree”

Design principle: keep the two traditions visually distinguishable while connecting them through a single story and composition.
